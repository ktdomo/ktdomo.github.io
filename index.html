Introduction

Katie Donnelly Moran

Sociologist of gender · Queer demographer · Inequality scholar

Postgraduate Research Associate, Department of Sociology
Princeton University

Hello! My name is Katie, and I use they/them pronouns. I recently earned my PhD in Sociology from Princeton University where I also completed a specialization on demography and a certificate in gender and sexuality studies. I am currently a postgraduate research associate at Princeton’s Department of Sociology. I am on the academic job market this year.

I study the labor market marginalization and health disparities of transgender populations, documenting how inequalities are patterned by gender embodiment, timing of transition, and race. To better quantitatively represent the experiences of this population, I develop new approaches to measure social difference, with implications for how complex social constructs and life course processes are studied generally. My Research Page describes my research projects and agenda.

Outside of work, I am an avid knitter and vegan foodie, and I enjoy combining both with travel. I love to discover new local yarns, and I am always on the lookout for vegan pastries and the best ice cream flavors. Photos of my knitting and favorite places to knit are here!
