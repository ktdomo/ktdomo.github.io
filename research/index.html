Research

Questions, evidence, and ongoing work.

My research examines gender classification, economic inequality, and social measurement through transgender experiences and quantitative methods.

Research directions

Three connected lines of inquiry.

01

Gender beyond identity

I use latent class analysis to study gender as interlocking individual, presentational, relational, and institutional dimensions of experience.

02

Transition and inequality

My work takes a life-course perspective on transgender income disparities, asking how the economic costs of transition differ across people and cohorts.

03

Measuring identity

I examine how surveys classify gender and sexual identity, including what open-ended ‘other’ responses reveal about measurement and categorization.
